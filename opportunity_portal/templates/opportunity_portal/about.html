About Opportunity Portal

Opportunity Portal is a platform designed to connect students, job seekers and organizations in one place.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make it easier for applicants to discover career opportunities and apply for jobs and internships.

For Applicants

Applicants can explore available opportunities, submit applications, bookmark jobs and manage their applications through their dashboard.

For Organizations

Organizations can publish opportunities and review applications submitted by potential candidates.
